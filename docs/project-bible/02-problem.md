# Problem Definition

- **Status:** Draft v1 — Awaiting human review
- **Owner:** Product Lead
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-19
- **Purpose:** Frame the product problem without prematurely choosing a behavioral explanation or solution.

## Confirmed facts

- The product selected for the graduation project is Zepto.
- The supplied business goal is to increase the percentage of Monthly Active Customers purchasing from at least one new category each month.
- The project will use publicly accessible research rather than primary interviews.
- No Zepto internal customer, transaction, or experimentation data is currently available.

## Problem frame

The project must determine what prevents or enables customers to expand beyond their established category repertoire during a month. The unknown is behavioral: when a need arises, why does a customer consider, ignore, delay, or purchase an unfamiliar category on Zepto or elsewhere?

Research must examine:

- Shopping missions and urgency
- Category consideration
- Existing habits
- Trust formation
- Perceived functional, financial, and emotional risk
- Information needs and decision criteria
- Triggers for experimentation
- Current workarounds
- Outcomes that reinforce or discourage future experimentation

## Research hypotheses, not conclusions

Possible mechanisms include narrow platform mental models, high trial risk, insufficient information, low relevance during urgent missions, or established cross-platform workarounds. These are hypotheses only. No mechanism has been confirmed.

## Problem boundaries

In scope:

- Behavioral causes of category consideration and experimentation
- Differences by shopping mission, category, and observable segment context
- Opportunities Zepto could plausibly influence
- Measurement of first-time and reactivated-category purchasing

Out of scope for Phase 1:

- Choosing or building an MVP feature
- Estimating production uplift
- Claiming market prevalence
- Building a personalization system
- Assessing internal Zepto strategy or performance

## Decision this research must enable

Which evidence-backed behavioral opportunity is both valuable to customers and capable of increasing meaningful category expansion without harming the core shopping mission?
