## Confirmed business goal

> Increase the percentage of Monthly Active Customers purchasing from at least one new category each month.

## Required metric interpretations

Both definitions remain active until human review and data feasibility determine their roles.

### A. Lifetime-new category

A customer purchases from a category for which they have no prior recorded purchase.

Open issues:

- Requires reliable purchase history and a stable category taxonomy.
- Customers eventually exhaust eligible categories.
- Historical data boundaries can falsely classify an old category as new.

### B. Dormant category

A customer purchases from a category not purchased within a defined lookback period.

Open issues:

- The lookback period is not yet decided.
- **180 days is a provisional operational assumption for evaluation only, not a fact or approved definition.**
- Appropriate dormancy may differ by natural category purchase frequency.

## Behavioral outcome

The intended change is not merely exposure or clicking. It is a completed category purchase that represents useful experimentation or renewed category engagement.

## Guardrail intent

Later product decisions should protect normal shopping completion, customer trust, purchase quality, and business sustainability. Exact guardrails require a future measurement review.

## Unresolved decisions

- Formal definition of Monthly Active Customer
- Category hierarchy level used for measurement
- Lifetime history boundary
- Dormant lookback period and whether it varies by category
- Whether cancelled or fully refunded orders count
- Whether repeat purchase is a success qualifier or secondary outcome
