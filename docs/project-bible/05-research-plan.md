# Research Plan

- **Status:** Draft v1.1 — Awaiting human review
- **Owner:** Research Lead
- **Version:** 1.1
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-19
- **Purpose:** Define a time-bounded public-research plan that produces traceable behavioral findings.

## Objective

Explain the behaviors, contexts, mental models, risks, information needs, and workarounds associated with category experimentation, then identify opportunities Zepto could plausibly influence.

Primary scope is category-level expansion. Product-level evidence is retained only when it explains entry, abandonment, purchase, purchase elsewhere, workaround, or repeat behavior for a lifetime-new or dormant category.

## Confirmed constraints

- Use publicly accessible sources only.
- Do not conduct or imply primary interviews.
- Do not invent evidence or prevalence.
- Do not select an MVP before the PDD and Opportunity Tree are reviewed.
- Keep research operations proportionate to a graduation project.

## Research sequence

1. Approve questions, hypotheses, taxonomy, codebook, source policy, and starter query matrix.
2. Run a small, diverse pilot collection.
3. Manually inspect relevance, context, duplication, and source-policy compliance.
4. Revise queries and codes once, based on the pilot.
5. Collect a bounded corpus with source and category coverage tracked.
6. Freeze the corpus version used for synthesis.
7. Extract atomic evidence and link it to behavioral concepts.
8. Seek contradictory cases before accepting an insight.
9. Build behavior models, JTBD, mental models, and the PDD.
10. Construct and prioritize the Opportunity Tree.

## Sampling approach

Use purposive, maximum-variation sampling rather than claiming statistical representation. Seek variation across source types, experience valence, categories, shopping missions, successful experiments, abandoned consideration, and Zepto-specific versus broader quick-commerce discussions.

### Provisional manual pilot

The following are **operational pilot assumptions**, not statistical sufficiency thresholds:

- Review 30–40 candidate sources manually.
- Retain approximately 20–25 evidence items that meet the protocol.
- Include at least 3 source types.
- Include at least 4 category groups.
- Include at least 5 contradictory or boundary cases.
- Keep every platform at or below 50% of retained evidence.
- Stop or revise early if relevance or context quality is poor.

Scale only after the pilot demonstrates useful evidence quality. These ranges may be revised during pilot review and do not establish representativeness.

## Evidence unit

The unit of analysis is a bounded behavioral observation with source, context, and interpretation—not an entire review and not a sentiment label.

## Outputs

- Reviewed corpus and evidence map
- Updated hypothesis register
- Behavioral themes and contradictions
- Habit loops, decision journeys, JTBD, and mental models
- Product Discovery Document
- Opportunity Solution Tree and Opportunity Briefs

## Lightweight checkpoints

- **Protocol review:** Is the study likely to answer the business question?
- **Pilot review:** Are sources relevant, ethical, diverse, and context-rich?
- **Corpus review:** Is the corpus adequate for directional synthesis?
- **Insight review:** Are conclusions traceable and appropriately bounded?

## Stop or revise conditions

Pause collection if sources repeatedly violate policy, queries mostly return generic sentiment, one platform dominates, duplication is high, or context is too weak to infer behavior responsibly.
