# Hypothesis Register

- **Status:** Draft v1 — Awaiting human review
- **Owner:** Product Research Lead
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-19
- **Purpose:** Track falsifiable explanations and update them as supporting and opposing evidence is collected.

## Register rules

- These are research hypotheses, not findings.
- Confidence is unassessed until evidence is reviewed.
- A hypothesis may be narrowed, rejected, or superseded.
- Product decisions must not cite an open hypothesis as evidence.

## Initial hypotheses

### H01 — Mission narrowness

- **ID:** H01
- **Statement:** During urgent or replenishment missions, customers are less likely to consider an unfamiliar category than during planned or exploratory missions.
- **Type:** Behavioral
- **Rationale:** Limited attention and a fixed task may reduce exploration; this is unverified.
- **Supporting evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Opposing evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Confidence:** Unassessed — no evidence collected.
- **Status:** Open hypothesis.
- **Next evidence required:** Accounts comparing category consideration across urgent, replenishment, planned, and exploratory missions.
- **Decision:** Do not use for product selection yet.

### H02 — Category-dependent perceived risk

- **ID:** H02
- **Statement:** Customers are less willing to experiment in categories where an unsuitable purchase is perceived to have meaningful safety, quality, or caregiving consequences.
- **Type:** Behavioral / perceived risk
- **Rationale:** The cost of a wrong choice may vary by category; this is unverified.
- **Supporting evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Opposing evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Confidence:** Unassessed — no evidence collected.
- **Status:** Open hypothesis.
- **Next evidence required:** Cross-category examples of attempted, completed, and rejected experiments with stated consequences.
- **Decision:** Do not use for product selection yet.

### H03 — Trust-signal gap

- **ID:** H03
- **Statement:** For some unfamiliar categories, platform trust alone is insufficient and customers seek product-, brand-, expert-, or community-level trust signals before purchase.
- **Type:** Trust
- **Rationale:** Trust may not transfer equally from grocery fulfillment to product suitability; this is unverified.
- **Supporting evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Opposing evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Confidence:** Unassessed — no evidence collected.
- **Status:** Open hypothesis.
- **Next evidence required:** Evidence showing which trust signals were sought, ignored, or sufficient in completed and abandoned journeys.
- **Decision:** Do not use for product selection yet.

### H04 — Information insufficiency

- **ID:** H04
- **Statement:** Customers abandon or move unfamiliar-category purchases elsewhere when the available information does not answer category-specific decision questions.
- **Type:** Information need
- **Rationale:** Product titles and basic attributes may be inadequate for unfamiliar decisions; this is unverified.
- **Supporting evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Opposing evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Confidence:** Unassessed — no evidence collected.
- **Status:** Open hypothesis.
- **Next evidence required:** Journeys linking a specific unanswered question to comparison, postponement, abandonment, or purchase elsewhere.
- **Decision:** Do not use for product selection yet.

### H05 — Established workaround

- **ID:** H05
- **Statement:** Customers use specialist marketplaces, search, communities, known people, or offline stores to resolve uncertainty before buying an unfamiliar category.
- **Type:** Workaround
- **Rationale:** Alternate channels may supply expertise, assortment, comparison, or reassurance; this is unverified.
- **Supporting evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Opposing evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Confidence:** Unassessed — no evidence collected.
- **Status:** Open hypothesis.
- **Next evidence required:** Descriptions of the workaround, the need it serves, and whether the final purchase occurs on Zepto or elsewhere.
- **Decision:** Do not use for product selection yet.

### H06 — Contextual trigger

- **ID:** H06
- **Statement:** Category experimentation is more likely when an unfamiliar category is connected to an active need or shopping mission than when it is presented without context.
- **Type:** Trigger / consideration
- **Rationale:** Relevance at the moment of need may create consideration; this is unverified.
- **Supporting evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Opposing evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Confidence:** Unassessed — no evidence collected.
- **Status:** Open hypothesis.
- **Next evidence required:** Successful and unsuccessful experiments with clear triggers and mission context.
- **Decision:** Do not use for product selection yet.

### H07 — Incentive without durable adoption

- **ID:** H07
- **Statement:** Price incentives can produce a first category purchase without producing repeat purchase when underlying trust, suitability, or information needs remain unresolved.
- **Type:** Behavioral / business
- **Rationale:** Trial and durable category adoption may be different outcomes; this is unverified.
- **Supporting evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Opposing evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Confidence:** Unassessed — no evidence collected.
- **Status:** Open hypothesis.
- **Next evidence required:** Accounts of incentive-led trials and their repeat or non-repeat outcomes.
- **Decision:** Do not use for product selection yet.

### H08 — Positive experience expands the platform mental model

- **ID:** H08
- **Statement:** A satisfactory first purchase in an unfamiliar category increases later willingness to consider Zepto for adjacent or dormant categories.
- **Type:** Mental model / habit
- **Rationale:** Successful outcomes may broaden perceived platform suitability; this is unverified.
- **Supporting evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Opposing evidence:** Not collected yet.
- **Confidence:** Unassessed — no evidence collected.
- **Status:** Open hypothesis.
- **Next evidence required:** Longitudinal or retrospective accounts connecting a first successful experiment to later category consideration.
