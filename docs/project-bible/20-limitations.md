# Limitations

- **Status:** Draft v1 — Awaiting human review
- **Owner:** Research Lead
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-19
- **Purpose:** State the boundaries that constrain interpretation, product decisions, and future claims.

## Current limitations

### No evidence collected

No public corpus has been collected or reviewed. The hypothesis register contains no findings, statistics, or validated behavioral models.

### Public research is not representative

Public posters are self-selecting. Source volume cannot be treated as customer prevalence, market share, or the distribution of Zepto's Monthly Active Customers.

### No primary interviews

The project cannot ask follow-up questions, verify identity, or clarify missing context. Ambiguous evidence must be excluded or given lower weight.

### No internal Zepto data

The project currently lacks customer history, category taxonomy, baseline metrics, funnel data, operational constraints, margin data, and experiment results. Business impact remains directional.

### Observational evidence cannot establish causality

Public discussions may suggest mechanisms but cannot prove that a barrier caused a purchase outcome. Future experiments would be required for causal claims.

### Identity and segment limits

Segments may use only explicit, relevant context. Demographics, household status, or intent must not be inferred from weak cues.

### Source availability and policy

Pages may change, disappear, restrict access, or prohibit automated retrieval. Source terms and permitted storage must be reviewed before collection.

### Language and geography undecided

Initial language, geography, and time windows are not approved. English-only research may omit important customer behavior.

### Taxonomy is provisional

Category definitions are research scaffolding and may not match Zepto's current production hierarchy. They must not be presented as official.

### Metric definitions unresolved

Monthly Active Customer, category level, lifetime history, dormant lookback, and valid completed purchase remain open decisions. The 180-day lookback is provisional only.

### Product and UX work not started

No PDD, Opportunity Tree, PRD, Zepto interface audit, product design, implementation, or validation exists yet.

## Reporting rule

Final outputs must separate observed evidence, synthesis, hypothesis, operational assumption, illustrative example, and simulated result. Limitations should appear in the report and deck, not only in this document.
