**Purpose:** Hold the human-reviewed synthesis that connects behavioural theory,
industry context, interviews, and scored review evidence.

## Evidence boundary

Do not add a conclusion until its reviewed evidence IDs, source mix, category
scope, and counterevidence are linked. This file is intentionally empty of
findings at project setup.

## Behaviour Knowledge Base

Use the header-only behavioural-theories, commerce-insights, industry-case-
studies, and research-papers templates only after manual review. Link a record
to an opportunity only with an explicit opportunity ID and controlled theme.
An aligned record can increase confidence; it cannot replace review evidence or
select an MVP automatically.
