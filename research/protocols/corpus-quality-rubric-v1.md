# Corpus Quality Rubric v1

- **Status:** Draft v1 — Awaiting human review
- **Owner:** Research Lead
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-19
- **Purpose:** Decide whether a pilot or bounded corpus is suitable for directional behavioral synthesis.

## Use

Score each dimension after the pilot and before corpus freeze:

- **0 — Unusable:** Material defect blocks responsible synthesis.
- **1 — Usable with limits:** Adequate for a bounded question; limitation must be explicit.
- **2 — Strong:** Sufficient variation, context, and traceability for the intended directional claim.

Scores do not create statistical validity. Record a short rationale and corrective action for every 0 or 1.

## Rubric

| Dimension | 0 — Unusable | 1 — Usable with limits | 2 — Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relevance | Most items are generic sentiment or unrelated operations complaints. | Many items relate to category behavior but some require filtering. | Most items describe consideration, decision, workaround, experiment, or outcome. |
| Source diversity | Evidence comes from one source type. | Two source types or weak variation in audience/context. | Multiple source types contribute independent behavioral contexts. |
| Category diversity | One category dominates without an intentional narrow scope. | Several categories appear but coverage is uneven. | Planned category groups are represented or gaps are explicitly scoped out. |
| Recency | Dates are missing or too old for the research question. | Mix of recent and older material with known limits. | Dates are traceable and the approved recency window is reasonably covered. |
| Duplication | Copied, syndicated, or repeated items materially inflate patterns. | Some duplicates remain but are identified and excluded from recurrence counts. | Canonical, textual, and narrative duplicates are controlled. |
| Spam and authenticity | Promotional, bot-like, or irrelevant content is common. | Suspect content is isolated but reduces usable volume. | Suspect content is rare, flagged, and excluded from synthesis. |
| Context richness | Items lack mission, reason, decision, or outcome. | Items support limited coding but leave material ambiguity. | Items commonly provide enough context to explain behavior and boundaries. |
| Platform concentration | One platform overwhelms all conclusions. | Concentration exists and claims are bounded to it. | No single platform silently determines cross-platform themes. |
| Traceability | Items cannot be linked to verifiable public sources. | Most items are traceable; gaps are excluded from accepted insights. | Every synthesis-eligible item has source, capture, and provenance metadata. |
| Contradictory coverage | Only confirming cases were collected. | Some negative cases exist but search was inconsistent. | Querying deliberately includes confirming, opposing, and boundary cases. |
| Policy compliance | Private, sensitive, excessive, or disallowed content is present. | Minor issues are quarantined and corrected before use. | Included material complies with the approved source policy. |

## Non-negotiable gates

A corpus cannot be frozen while any of these score 0:

- Traceability
- Policy compliance
- Relevance
- Duplication

Contradictory coverage must score at least 1 before candidate insights are reviewed.

## Lightweight corpus report

Record only:

- Corpus version and freeze date
- Included and excluded item counts
- Breakdown by source type and category
- Duplicate and spam exclusions
- Rubric scores with one-line rationale
- Known gaps and claims those gaps prohibit
- Human approval or revision decision

## Interpretation rule

Item counts describe the collected corpus only. They must not be presented as customer prevalence or market statistics.
